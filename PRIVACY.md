# Privacy Policy — Writing Checker

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

This policy explains what Writing Checker collects, why, and what you can do
about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

> want users and Google to reach you at. Play requires a working contact.

## What the app collects

**The writing you submit.** When you check a piece of writing, its text — or a
photo or PDF of it — is sent to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's
Claude API for marking. **We do not store your writing on our server.** It is
held in memory for the length of the request and then discarded. What we do keep
is the word count, which tier you used, the token counts and what the request
cost us, so we can bill fairly and watch our own costs.

**Your saved checks stay on your phone.** The corrections and feedback you see
are stored only in your device's local storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Your language settings.** Your native language and the language you are
learning are stored on your device and sent with each check so the feedback
comes back in a language you understand.

**Your email address, only if you sign in.** Signing in with Google or Apple is
optional and exists so your credits survive a reinstall. If you do, we store the
provider's identifier for you and the email address it gives us. Nothing else
from your Google or Apple account is requested or stored.

**Purchase records.** When you buy credits, we store the store's transaction
identifier, which product it was, and how many credits it granted. We never see
or store your card details — payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the
App Store.

**A device identifier we generate.** On first launch the app creates a random
id so the server can hold a credit balance for your install. It is not your
device's advertising id, hardware id, or anything that identifies you elsewhere.

## What the app does not collect

- No card or payment details
- No location, contacts, photos library, or microphone
- No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics SDKs
- No account information beyond the email you approve at sign-in

## Who else sees your data

**Anthropic (Claude API).** Your submitted writing is sent to Anthropic to be
marked. It is processed under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not train
models on API inputs. See <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy>.

**Google Play / Apple.** They process your payment and give us a receipt to
verify. We never receive your payment details.

That is the complete list. Your data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone
else.

## Automated processing and AI

Corrections, explanations, scores and level estimates are produced by a large
language model. **They can be wrong.** Treat the feedback as a well-informed
suggestion from a tutor, not as an authoritative grade — particularly for exam
preparation or anything that matters formally.

## Deleting your account and data

In the app: **Settings → Your account → Delete my account**.

This immediately removes your email address and sign-in from our server and
releases the device registration, so the next launch starts a fresh account.
Saved checks on your phone are deleted at the same time.

Two things deliberately survive, and neither identifies you: the record that a
particular store receipt was already used — without it, the same receipt could
be redeemed again — and the anonymous ledger rows showing credits were granted
and spent, which we keep as a financial record.

**Any credits you have not spent are lost when you delete.** This cannot be
undone.

To request deletion without using the app, email `CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE` from the
address you signed in with.

## Children

Writing Checker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Security

Your writing travels over HTTPS. The device token that holds your balance is
kept in the platform keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), not in plain
storage. The Anthropic API key lives only on our server and never ships inside
the app.
